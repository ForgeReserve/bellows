An AI gateway its operator cannot read.

Bellows runs sealed. The person operating it cannot read your prompts or answers, and cannot see who paid. It speaks the OpenAI API and takes eCash per request, so your apps and agents need no account.

Developers GitHub

  • The operator cannot read it

    A sealed Bellows keeps your prompts and answers inside hardware its operator cannot open. Your app checks the build before it sends anything.

    What it hides, and its limits

  • Nobody sees who paid

    You pay with eCash, which carries no name and needs no account. Requests paid from one balance can still be linked to each other.

  • Run your own
