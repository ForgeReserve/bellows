# Code of conduct

Bellows is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. This page says how we treat each other in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space.

## What we ask of everyone

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Talk about the work. Criticise code and ideas, and leave the person out of it.
- Welcome newcomers. A first question or a first pull request deserves a helpful answer.
- Give reasons with a review comment, and take review as help.
- Respect privacy. Share someone's personal details only with their consent.

## What is out of bounds

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about who someone is.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Continuing after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in a project space concerns you, write to security@forge-reserve.com. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. Say what happened, where, and include a link if there is one.

## What the maintainers do

The maintainers look into each report and answer the person who made it. Depending on what happened, they may ask for a change in behaviour, remove a comment, or block someone from the project for a time or for good.
