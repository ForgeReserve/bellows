# Bellows brand files

The Bellows mark is two rings around a closed lock. The gold ring and the green ring are the rings of the Forge AI app (money and memory); the lock is the sealed hardware Bellows runs in.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `bellows-mark-dark.svg` | The mark on a dark background (transparent, white lock) |
| `bellows-mark-light.svg` | The mark on a light background (transparent, dark lock) |
| `bellows-icon.svg`, `bellows-icon.png` | App or avatar icon, 1024 x 1024, on the dark square |
| `social-preview.svg`, `social-preview.png` | Link preview, 1280 x 640; the SVG needs the Inter typeface installed to render its text |

Colours: gold `#E2B552` and green `#3FCFB4` on dark `#0B0D10`, with the lock in `#F5F5F7`. On light backgrounds: gold `#C9952F`, green `#0E7C6B`, lock `#14161A`.

The mark is drawn on a 120-unit grid with a 5-unit round stroke (6 in the icon). Keep space around it at least as wide as the gap between the two rings, and do not recolour, stretch or rotate it.
