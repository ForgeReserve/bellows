Backups and recovery

What protects the money when there is no database export.

The operator cannot read the volume, so there is no database export from a sealed deployment. What protects the money:

  • the offline copies of the volume key and the wallet secret (step 2);
  • the volume itself, which survives Stop, Deploy, updates and deleting the instance, until you delete the volume;
  • the mint, from which the wallet secret restores the gateway's eCash.

If the volume is lost, the eCash in the gateway's wallet can be restored with the wallet secret, and the balances customers held are lost. Clients that keep their balances small, and move them with moveBalance (Operators), lose little.

The volume has no rollback protection: whoever controls the disk can put back an older copy, and the enclave does not notice. See Threat model.
