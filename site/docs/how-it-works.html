How a request flows

One request from your app, through a relay, to a sealed Bellows and on to the model.

Step by step

  1. Your app reads the engine's attested identity and checks the hardware attestation and the build against builds.json. If the check fails, it sends nothing.
  2. It encrypts the API call to the attested key with Oblivious HTTP and posts it to a relay.
  3. The relay forwards the ciphertext. It sees your address and the ciphertext only.
  4. Inside the confidential VM, Bellows opens the request and takes payment: a balance key, or a Cashu token it swaps at the mint.
  5. Bellows calls the model, charges what the request used and streams the answer back the same way.

Each step in detail: Attestation for step 1, Oblivious HTTP for steps 2 and 3, Payments for step 4, and the guide for all of them in code.
